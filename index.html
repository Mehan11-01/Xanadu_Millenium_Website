PUCHONG LOCATION

Prime Address

Puchong is a well-established township offering a convenient balance of modern city living and comfortable residential surroundings. Strategically located in Puchong Perdana, residents enjoy excellent connectivity via the LRT network, major highways and a covered walkway directly to LRT Puchong Perdana.

Surrounded by shopping malls, schools, dining and everyday amenities, Puchong is an ideal location for both homeowners and property investors.
